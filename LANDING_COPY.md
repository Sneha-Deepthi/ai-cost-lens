# FAQ

## 1. What does AI Cost Lens actually analyze?

AI Cost Lens analyzes AI tooling subscriptions, estimated spend, overlapping workflows, and optimization opportunities across products like ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor, Copilot, Gemini, Windsurf, and Lovable.

---

## 2. Does this connect directly to billing providers?

No. The current version is recommendation-driven and based on user-provided tooling information rather than direct billing integrations.

---

## 3. Are the savings estimates guaranteed?

No. Savings estimates are directional recommendations based on publicly verified pricing data and workflow overlap assumptions. Actual savings depend on team usage patterns and operational needs.

---

## 4. Is my company information publicly visible?

No. Public audit reports intentionally exclude identifying information such as company names and email addresses.
