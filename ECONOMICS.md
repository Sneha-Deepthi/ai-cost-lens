# Economics

## Overview

AI Cost Lens is designed as a lead-generation and qualification layer for Credex. The product itself is free, but its economic value comes from identifying startups with meaningful AI infrastructure spend and converting them into:
- consulting opportunities,
- AI credit customers,
- or infrastructure optimization clients.

The core assumption is that companies already spending heavily on AI tooling are likely candidates for:
- cloud credits,
- vendor negotiations,
- infrastructure optimization,
- and centralized procurement support.

---

# What Is a Converted Lead Worth to Credex?

To estimate lead value, I modeled a realistic early-stage startup customer.

Typical target customer:
- 10–30 engineers
- multiple AI subscriptions
- $1k–$10k/month AI tooling spend
- growing API usage

Assume Credex monetizes through:
- AI credit partnerships,
- procurement facilitation,
- or optimization consulting.

Estimated customer economics:

| Stage | Estimated Value |
|---|---|
| Consultation booked | $0 immediate revenue |
| Optimization engagement | $1,000–$5,000 one-time |
| AI credit or infrastructure partnership | $3,000–$15,000 ARR equivalent |
| Long-term infrastructure relationship | $10k–$50k+ lifetime value |

For modeling purposes, I assume:

```text
Average converted customer value ≈ $8,000
```

This is intentionally conservative for B2B infrastructure-adjacent tooling.

# Conversion Funnel Assumptions

The business only works if audit generation converts into higher-intent conversations.

Estimated funnel:

| Funnel Stage | Conversion Rate |
|---|---|
| Audit completed → lead capture | 20% |
| Lead capture → consultation booked | 25% |
| Consultation booked → paid customer | 20% |

Example funnel math:

```text
1,000 completed audits
→ 200 leads
→ 50 consultations
→ 10 paying customers
```

Using the earlier assumption:

```text
10 customers × $8,000 average value
= $80,000 total value
```

If acquisition costs remain mostly organic, this becomes highly profitable even at moderate conversion rates.

# What Must Be True to Reach $1M ARR in 18 Months?

Assume Credex monetizes customers at:

```text
$8,000 average annual value
```

To reach:

```text
$1,000,000 ARR
```

the platform would need approximately:

```text
125 active paying customers
```

Now estimate funnel backwards.

Assume:
- 20% audit → lead conversion
- 25% lead → consultation conversion
- 20% consultation → customer conversion

Combined conversion:

```text
0.20 × 0.25 × 0.20
= 1%
```

That means:

```text
12,500 completed audits
→ 125 customers
```

Spread across 18 months:

```text
~695 audits/month
~23 audits/day
```

This is a surprisingly achievable number if:
- public audit sharing works,
- startup communities distribute reports organically,
- and Credex already has relevant infrastructure partnerships.

The model becomes especially attractive because:
- audit generation is low-cost,
- acquisition can remain mostly organic,
- and public reports create compounding distribution.

# Key Economic Insight

The strongest economic advantage of AI Cost Lens is that it qualifies high-intent infrastructure buyers before any sales conversation happens.

Most B2B lead funnels rely on:
- demos,
- cold outreach,
- or content marketing.

This product instead:
- quantifies pain immediately,
- visualizes savings,
- and turns optimization into a shareable artifact.

That dramatically reduces trust-building time and improves sales efficiency.

The real value is not the audit itself.

The value is identifying startups already spending heavily on AI infrastructure and entering the conversation before procurement workflows mature.
