# Plans

## Individual

Price:
- $10/month

Best For:
- solo developers
- students
- individual engineers

Features:
- AI code completion
- chat assistance
- IDE integration

---

## Business

Price:
- $19/user/month

Best For:
- engineering teams

Features:
- organization management
- policy controls
- centralized billing

Potential Overkill Indicators:
- teams under 3 users
- no governance requirements

Possible Downgrade:
- Individual plan

---

## Enterprise

Price:
- $39/user/month

Best For:
- large enterprises

Features:
- advanced governance
- enterprise security
- audit controls

Potential Overkill Indicators:
- startups without compliance requirements

---

# Audit Logic Ideas

- Small engineering teams on Enterprise may be overpaying for governance tooling
- Individual developers may not benefit from Business features
- Multiple coding AI subscriptions may introduce overlapping spend
