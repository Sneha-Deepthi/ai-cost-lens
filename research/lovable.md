# Overview

Lovable is an AI-powered application builder focused on:
- rapid MVP generation
- AI-assisted full-stack development
- startup prototyping
- frontend and backend scaffolding
- Supabase-centric workflows

The platform is commonly used by:
- indie hackers
- startup founders
- rapid product teams
- non-traditional developers

Lovable is often compared with:
- v0
- Bolt.new
- Replit AI
- Cursor

# Lovable Plans

## Free

Price:
- $0/month

Best For:
- experimentation
- evaluating workflows
- hobby projects

Features:
- 5 daily credits
- up to 30 monthly credits
- public projects
- lightweight prototyping

Limitations:
- restricted AI generations
- limited scaling capability

Potential Audit Insights:
- suitable for lightweight experimentation but insufficient for production workflows

---

## Pro

Price:
- $25/month

Best For:
- indie hackers
- solo founders
- freelancers
- rapid MVP builders

Features:
- 100 monthly credits
- up to 150 monthly daily credits
- expanded app generation
- AI-assisted full-stack workflows
- Supabase integrations

Strengths:
- strong balance between capability and pricing
- optimized for rapid product iteration

Potential Audit Insights:
- often the most cost-efficient tier for solo product builders

---

## Business

Price:
- $50/month

Best For:
- startup teams
- collaborative product teams
- growing engineering organizations

Features:
- all Pro features
- 100 monthly credits
- internal publishing
- SSO
- collaborative workflows

Strengths:
- stronger governance and collaboration support
- centralized startup workflows

Potential Overkill Indicators:
- solo developers
- teams with minimal collaboration requirements

Possible Downgrade Path:
- Lovable Pro

Potential Audit Insights:
- small teams may not fully utilize collaboration and governance capabilities

---

## Enterprise

Pricing:
- platform-fee-based custom pricing

Best For:
- large organizations
- enterprise prototyping workflows
- governance-heavy environments

Features:
- volume-based credit pricing
- dedicated support
- enterprise governance
- scalable organizational workflows

Potential Audit Insights:
- enterprise pricing should align with actual governance and organizational complexity requirements

# Audit Logic Ideas

## Rule 1

If:
- solo founder
- using Lovable Business

Recommendation:
- evaluate Lovable Pro

Reason:
- collaboration and governance tooling may be unnecessary for solo product workflows.

---

## Rule 2

If:
- paying for Lovable + Cursor + Copilot simultaneously

Recommendation:
- review overlap between AI development tools

Reason:
- multiple AI-assisted development subscriptions may duplicate functionality.

---

## Rule 3

If:
- organization primarily uses conversational AI
- minimal product development workflows

Recommendation:
- reconsider dedicated AI prototyping subscriptions

Reason:
- specialized AI app-building workflows may not align with operational usage.

---

## Rule 4

If:
- startup relies heavily on rapid MVP iteration

Recommendation:
- continue Lovable usage

Reason:
- rapid prototyping workflows may justify operational cost through faster product iteration.

---

## Rule 5

If:
- organization requires governance or centralized collaboration

Recommendation:
- continue Business or Enterprise plans

Reason:
- governance and collaboration tooling may justify operational spend.

# Key Product Positioning Insights

Lovable is strongest for:
- rapid startup prototyping
- AI-assisted app generation
- MVP workflows
- Supabase-centric development
- product experimentation

Lovable is weaker for:
- enterprise-grade engineering
- deep IDE-native coding assistance
- advanced low-level software engineering

The platform becomes most cost-efficient when:
- organizations prioritize rapid iteration
- teams consolidate overlapping prototyping tools
- collaboration needs align with Business-tier capabilities
